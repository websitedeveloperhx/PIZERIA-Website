Why PIZERIA

  • Built to order

    Nothing sits waiting. Your pizza is assembled once you order it.

  • Wood-fired heat

    A very hot oven gives the crust its blistered, leopard-spotted edge.

  • Hand-stretched

    Dough is shaped by hand, so every crust is a little different.

  • Made for sharing

    Cut into generous slices and meant for the middle of the table.

The art of good pizza

A good pizza is mostly patience: dough that's had time, a hot oven, and a few things on top that earn their place.
